## Planned Safe Baseline Checks

- Canonical AI guardrail preflight in no-run mode.
- Change-packet structural validation, followed by strict campaign-only coverage
  analysis that separately reports unrelated dirty files.
- Generated-context checks appropriate to audit documentation changes.
- Targeted validations selected only after coverage rows identify the behavior
  being examined.

## Separately Gated Evidence

Representative real-media validation and live mutation routes are not part of
initial discovery. Their absence must remain visible in the final readiness
assessment unless a later, explicitly isolated gate is satisfied.
